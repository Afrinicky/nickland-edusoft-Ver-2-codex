Why schools move to Edusoft

One record of the school, kept straight.

Most schools keep the roll in one book, the fees in another, the marks in a teacher's exercise book, and the answer to "how much is this child owing" in somebody's head. Edusoft is the one place all of it lives, so a bill, a report card and a debtor list all agree.

It was written for Ghanaian pre-tertiary schools — Nursery through JHS — and it bills the way those schools actually bill: one school fees bill per pupil per term, extras added to the same bill, books once a year, arrears carried forward automatically.

How access works

Nobody sees what is not theirs.

Every member of staff is given a level on each module — no access, view, contribute, manage or full. A subject teacher entering marks for their own class cannot open the payroll, and cannot reach another class's marks by typing its address.

That is enforced by the system, not by hiding the button. Refusals are written to the school's own audit trail, which the head teacher can read.

Questions schools ask

Which pupils am I billed for?

Pupils whose record is active. A pupil who has been withdrawn, has graduated, or whose record is archived is still in your database and is not counted.

What happens when the trial ends?

You are told before it ends. On the day it ends your subscription starts on the plan you chose, and the first invoice is raised for the pupils on your roll at that moment.

What happens if a payment fails?

Nothing, at first — you are simply told, and nothing changes. After that there is a grace period. Only then is access limited, and even then nothing of yours is ever deleted; it all comes back the moment the bill is settled.

Can we pay by mobile money or bank transfer?

Yes. Card and mobile money are handled at checkout. For a bank transfer or a termly invoice, write to us and we will set it up on your account.

About

Built for the schools that use it.

Nickland Edusoft is made by Nickland Sales for Ghanaian pre-tertiary schools — Nursery, Primary and JHS.

It began as an office system that runs on one computer, with no internet, because that is the situation most schools are actually in. That has not changed: the school's own system still holds the school's records, and still works when the network does not.

What the platform adds is reach. A teacher can mark a register from a phone. A parent can see a receipt without coming to the office. A head teacher can look at the term's collections from home. And a school can start using all of it by registering on this page, with nothing to install.

We bill per pupil because a school of sixty pupils and a school of six hundred are not the same customer, and asking both for the same licence fee prices the smaller one out of a system it needs more.

How your school's data is kept

Every school on this platform has its own database. Not a shared table with a column saying which school a row belongs to — its own database, so one school's query cannot reach another school's pupils even if somebody writes it wrongly.

Card details are never stored by us. They are held by the payment provider, and what we keep is a reference that can be charged and nothing that can be read.

Administrative actions on the platform — a plan changed, a discount granted, a school suspended — are written to an audit trail with who did it and when.
